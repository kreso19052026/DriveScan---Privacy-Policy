Privacy Policy

App: DriveScan — OBD2 Diagnostics (com.kozotechnology.drivescan)
Developer: Kozo Technology
Effective date: October 23, 2026

DriveScan ("the App", "we", "us") is a car diagnostics app. It connects to an ELM327-compatible OBD-II adapter in your car and shows live sensor data, fault codes, vehicle information, trip and fuel figures and performance-run results. There is no account and no sign-in, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

Data that stays on your device

  • Vehicle data read from your car: VIN, fault codes, sensor values, readiness, mileage and module information.
  • Garage: the cars you add, their details, licence plate, photo, service log and reminders.
  • Performance runs: speed and RPM samples, the GPS route of the run, the result, notes and the weather at the time of the run.
  • Trips and data logs: distance, fuel use, cost and recorded sensor values.
  • Settings, such as units, theme, fuel price and the selected adapter.

This data is stored only in the App's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us. Files you export (PDF, CSV, Excel, JSON, text, images) are created when you ask for them and handed to the Android share sheet or the file picker; you decide where they go.

Location

The App uses your phone's GPS only while it is open: to draw the route of a performance run, to warn about fixed speed cameras during a run and to show your position on the live map. The App never requests background location.

After a run, the App sends the start point of the run, rounded to about 100 m, and the date to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to add the weather to the run report. No identifiers are sent.

Other network requests

  • Maps: map images are loaded from the OpenStreetMap tile server (tile.openstreetmap.org), which receives the map area being shown and your IP address.
  • Speed camera list: the list of fixed speed cameras is downloaded from public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, maps.mail.ru) about once a day. The request asks for a whole country and does not contain your position.
  • Web search: if you tap "Search causes & fixes" on a fault code, your browser opens a Google search for that code and your car's make.
  • Wi-Fi adapters are reached on your local network only.

Advertising and your consent

The App shows native ads and an optional rewarded video ad (watch one to use the App without ads for 24 hours) from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not sell any data and we receive no personal data from AdMob.

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the App asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads.

United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.

Change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy → Privacy options.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: find and connect to your OBD-II adapter. Bluetooth scan results are not used to find your location.
  • Location (while the App is open): route of performance runs, speed camera warnings and the live map.
  • Internet: Wi-Fi adapters, maps, weather, the speed camera list and ads.
  • Camera / photos (optional): a photo of your car in the Garage, taken with your camera app or picked with the system photo picker. It stays on your phone.

Data retention and deletion

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete cars, service entries, runs and logs in the App. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all of it. Because we have no server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
